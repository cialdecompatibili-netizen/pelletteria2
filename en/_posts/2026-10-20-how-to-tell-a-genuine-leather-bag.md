A good leather bag lasts for years and gets better with use. Before you choose, here are five simple checks you can make even in the shop.

## 1. Look at the edges

On a cut edge, genuine leather shows a fibrous, slightly uneven surface. A synthetic material has a smooth, uniform edge, often with a visible layer of plastic.

## 2. Touch and smell

Leather is soft, warms up to the touch and has a distinctive smell, never chemical or plastic-like. If you press it with a finger, a small wrinkle forms and then fades away.

## 3. Look at the grain

In real leather the grain is never perfectly identical from one point to another. A pattern that is too regular, repeating exactly, is almost always embossed.

## 4. Check the stitching and finishing

Straight, tight, even stitching, neatly finished corners and a clean lining are signs of careful workmanship. These are the details that make the difference after a few years.

## 5. Choose according to how you use it

- **Everyday use:** full-grain leather is best: hard-wearing and easy to care for.
- **Elegant and evening wear:** smooth, thin leather, for clutches and small bags.
- **Travel and work:** rigid structures and reinforced stitching, with a well-balanced weight.

## How to look after it

Keep the bag away from heat sources and damp, stuff it with paper when you are not using it, and condition it a couple of times a year with a dedicated product.
